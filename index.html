    Frequently asked questions

    Quick answers before or after you watch the webinar.

    A Transaction Certificate (TC) is a document issued by a certification body that confirms a specific shipment of certified material was sold and delivered by a certified supplier.

    Apply for the TC immediately after shipment and provide it within the timeline set out in the TC policy covered in the webinar.

    Delayed or missing TCs can lead to compliance issues and may affect the certified status of the related goods. Contact your Otto International representative as early as possible.
